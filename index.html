    User Manual

    Everything you need to play Avatar Chess — rules, powers, turns, and saves. Prefer learning on the board?

    Goal

    Eliminate every rival pawn. The last player with at least one pawn wins.

    Avatar morph

    Pieces look like pawns until it is your turn — then they transform into your current power. After you move, they become pawns again.

    →
    Rook

    Setup

    Each player has 2 pawns on their home edge (rotationally symmetric opening). Seats run clockwise with A at the bottom of the screen:

    • A (blue) — bottom · rank 8: b8, c8
    • B (red) — left · file h: h6, h7
    • C (green) — top · rank 1: f1, g1
    • D (orange) — right · file a: a2, a3

    How powers move

    ♖ Rook — any number of squares along a row or column (blocked by pieces).

    ♘ Knight — L-shaped jump (2 + 1). Jumps over pieces.

    ♗ Bishop — any number of squares diagonally (blocked by pieces).

    Powers (staggered each round)

    Each living player moves once per round. When the round ends, powers rotate.

    A B C D

    Powers rotate every round (wizard FX in-game)

    Round A B C D
    1 Rook Knight Bishop Rook
    2 Knight Bishop Rook Knight
    3 Bishop Rook Knight Bishop

    Round 4 returns to the Round 1 pattern, and so on.

    A turn

    1 Morph
    2 Select
    3 Move / capture
    1. Your pawns morph into your current power.
    2. Select one of your pieces; legal squares light up.
    3. Move there. Landing on an opponent captures them. After the move, pieces return to pawns.

    Shrink rings (optional)

    When Shrink rings is On, each empty edge of the playable area is cut away (top, bottom, left, or right independently). Play stays inside the remaining rectangle down to 2×2. Classic win still applies — last avatar standing.

    Humans vs PC

    On the welcome screen choose 1–3 human players; remaining seats are PC. During a match you can tap a seat in the sidebar power list to toggle Human / PC.

    Live training

    Train starts a guided match on the real board. The coach highlights the pieces you may use, blocks other clicks, and walks you through Rook → Knight → Bishop → Rook → power rotate. Training is not autosaved.
